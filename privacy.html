Privacy Policy

StudySprint AI · Effective October 13, 2026

About the app

StudySprint AI is an independently developed learning tool that runs within the Anna platform. It uses Anna's AI functionality to generate quizzes, flashcards and study plans from the text you enter.

Information you provide

When you use the app, you may paste study notes or topic explanations and choose settings such as difficulty and question count. The app sends your entered notes and selected settings to Anna's AI service to generate the requested material. Do not enter sensitive personal information, passwords, private records, or confidential content.

How information is used

Your submitted material is used to prepare your selected study activity and return generated content to the app. The current StudySprint AI code does not include an independent database, user-account system, or analytics service operated by its developer. This does not mean Anna or other platform providers collect no data.

Platform processing and retention

Anna provides the app's runtime and AI services. Data may be processed and retained under Anna's applicable policies and account settings. StudySprint AI's developer does not control Anna's platform-level logging, retention or security practices. Consult Anna's current privacy information for details about platform processing.

Website

This informational website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The site does not include developer-added tracking scripts or analytics. GitHub may process technical information about visits according to its own policies.

Support requests

Support is provided through GitHub Issues. Issues may be publicly visible and are subject to GitHub's policies. Do not include sensitive data in issue reports.

Your choices

You may choose not to enter notes, close the app, or remove the installed Anna app. For questions about Anna account data or platform-level deletion, contact Anna through its official support channels. For questions about this app's implementation, use the support page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date on this page will be updated when the policy changes.
